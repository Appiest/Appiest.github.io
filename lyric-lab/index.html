What each section is for

Verse

Moves the story. New information every time: who, where, what happened. Specific nouns live here.

Pre-chorus

The ramp. Shorter lines, rising tension, a feeling that something has to be said.

Chorus

The thesis. Repeated, so it has to survive repetition. The best ones mean more each time they return.

Bridge

A new angle. A different speaker, a confession, a time jump. It earns the last chorus.

Outro

Release or echo. Kahan often uses it as the biggest, loudest moment instead of a fade.
